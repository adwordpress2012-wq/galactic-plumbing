# Galactic Plumbing SCW Flow

## Customer Micah Flow
1. Customer opens Micah from bottom-right floating widget.
2. Micah greets: “Hi, I’m Micah. I can take your booking while Luke is on-site. How can I help today?”
3. Micah collects:
   - name
   - phone
   - suburb
   - plumbing issue
   - urgency
   - preferred day/time
4. Micah confirms details back to customer.
5. Micah sends booking/enquiry notification to Luke.
6. Customer receives confirmation message that Luke will respond ASAP.

## Important Rules
- Micah captures booking requests, not final confirmed jobs unless DOS Calendar confirmation is wired.
- Micah does not give pricing.
- Micah does not promise exact arrival times.
- Luke personally handles quotes and onsite work.
